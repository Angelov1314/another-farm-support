Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Thank you for playing Another Farm. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address for authentication purposes.
  • Game Data: Your farm progress, collections, and in-game activity are stored to sync your experience across devices.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics such as session length and feature usage to improve the game experience.
  • Purchase History: Records of in-app purchases made through Apple's payment system.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your game progress across devices
  • To authenticate your account via Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple
  • To process and restore in-app purchases
  • To improve gameplay features and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

3. Data Storage

Your game data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud). Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Another Farm uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and cloud data storage — Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple: Authentication — Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication — Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

Another Farm is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at herojerryyang@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
