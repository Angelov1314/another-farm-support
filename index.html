Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my progress?

Your progress is automatically saved to the cloud when you sign in with Google or Apple. If you play as a guest, your data is stored locally on your device.

Can I play on multiple devices?

Yes! Sign in with Google or Apple to sync your farm across devices.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to the in-game shop and tap "Restore Purchases" to recover any previous purchases.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

  • Make sure your app is updated to the latest version.
  • Restart the app.
  • If the issue persists, please contact us with your device model and OS version.
